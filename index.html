Final year project · 2026–27

BowlSense

A cricket delivery is legal only if the bowler's elbow straightens by 15° or less. Measuring that needs a motion-capture lab. BowlSense does it from one smartphone video, and refuses to answer when the video can't support one.

  1. 2D poseFine-tuned YOLO26-Pose, refined with optical flow
  2. Event detectionTCN ensemble finds arm-horizontal and ball release
  3. 3D liftingMotionAGFormer / PoseBERT, fine-tuned
  4. Elbow extensionAngle at release minus the minimum before it
  5. Accept or rejectAn uncertainty score decides, and says why
  • 0deliveries from 14 bowlers
  • 0keypoint mAP50-95 (YOLO26x)
  • 0frame mean error on arm-horizontal
  • 0of 52 held-out events within 12.5 ms

I own the API and client: labelling tool, event detector training, FastAPI server, Flutter web and Docker. The 3D lifting and uncertainty stages land between Dec 2026 and May 2027. Supervised by Ms. Noor Ul Ain.
